# Maple weekly update

## Current plan

The Maple pilot remains scheduled to open on October 29 and will focus on accessibility and independent use in a reading-room setting for 30 volunteer readers [obsidian-e220e550812487bf-0F5][obsidian-9199248a1040f184-0F1]. The pilot will keep assisted checkout available throughout the trial, and the printed quick-start guide must use at least 16-point type [obsidian-2cf813a1faf7c001-0F1][obsidian-2cf813a1faf7c001-0F2].

## Rehearsal evidence

During the most recent rehearsal, 26 of 30 volunteers completed checkout without assistance, meeting the success threshold of at least 24 independent checkouts [obsidian-e220e550812487bf-0F1][obsidian-9199248a1040f184-0F3]. Four readers asked for clearer shelf labels, highlighting a specific area for improvement [obsidian-e220e550812487bf-0F2].

## Blocker and next action

Unclear shelf labels remain the main blocker for the pilot [obsidian-e220e550812487bf-0F3]. The team will review replacement labels on October 23 to address this issue before the pilot opens [obsidian-e220e550812487bf-0F4].

## Pilot opening versus public launch

The October 29 date marks the pilot opening for a defined group of readers, not a public launch. The team will review accessibility feedback before making any public launch commitment [obsidian-2cf813a1faf7c001-0F3].
