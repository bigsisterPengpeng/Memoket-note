# Turn scattered notes into your next draft.

[Download the narrated MP4](agent-native-writing-workspace.mp4?raw=1) · [Captions](agent-native-writing-workspace.en.vtt) · [Inline preview](writing-workflow.gif)

Real application interactions and live GPT-4.1 output with fictional Maple sources. Captures are sampled, waits are shortened, and some views are cropped for readability. English narration is synthetic. UI interactions are automated to demonstrate the workflow and review controls. This is an edited demonstration, not an uninterrupted screen recording. No private recordings or personal Notion/Apple Notes accounts are accessed. Image generation is shown as a menu option; the demonstrated figure action is data visualization.

## 00:00:00 Turn scattered notes into your next draft.

Memoket Note. An agent-native writing workspace. Bring your notes in. Work with AI inside the document. Take a reviewed draft back to your workspace.

## 00:00:09 1 / Bring the notes you already have

Start with three fictional Maple project notes. Import creates local notes and extracts fourteen facts. Markdown, Obsidian, Notion and Apple Notes are supported sources.

## 00:00:20 2 / Remember across sessions

The knowledge survives a backend restart. Open a saved fact and inspect its dated original words. The relevant past appears beside the draft, without leaving the editor.

## 00:00:31 3 / Select. Polish. Review.

Select text and click Polish or Rewrite. The workspace provides the context. Review the marked changes: reject an unsupported causal claim, then keep a focused grammar improvement. You stay in the document.

## 00:00:44 4 / Figures and tables, right where you write

Type slash, or right-click without a selection, to insert AI writing, figures or tables. Here, Data visualization reads the existing table, calls real tools and inserts a rendered chart. Review its explanation against the source.

## 00:00:59 One passage, or a checked writing task?

Continue writes the next passage once, using relevant memory. Smart Continue works toward a goal through multiple writing and checking rounds. Choose the goal, audience and done criteria, then add evidence to Materials.

## 00:01:12 5 / Let the harness plan, write and check

The writing agent harness retrieves context, drafts and checks the result. Its plan, rounds and changes remain inspectable. This live run cites ten saved facts. You can pause and review each round.

## 00:01:25 6 / Review against the original evidence

A check is not a guarantee of truth. The reviewer removes a repeated title and corrects an unsupported timing claim. A citation opens the source evidence and the backlink to the draft.

## 00:01:35 7 / Take the reviewed draft back

Download Markdown without an account, or export into a separate Obsidian vault. The reviewed draft and its ten source identifiers are preserved. Configured Notion and Feishu exports are also available.

## 00:01:48 Your notes. Your memory. Your next draft.

Memoket Note is a personal open-source project built on the Memoket KITE memory engine. One-click AI, memory beside your draft, and an agent harness working toward your goal.
