# Preserve detail while polishing

Preserve verifiable names, numbers, dates and causal attribution when rewriting or polishing. Smoother language must not turn concrete information into vague generalities. Remove stock openings, excessive parallelism and artificial both-sides balance without leaving editorial commentary. Inspired by source-check and top-edit.
