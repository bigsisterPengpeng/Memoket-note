# Avoid automatic prose patterns

Avoid ending every paragraph with parallel clauses, inflating importance through not-only/but-also constructions, or forcing not-A-but-B contrasts without a substantive distinction. Vary sentence length naturally instead of producing only medium-length compound sentences. Inspired by llm-writing.
