# Distinguish evidence strength

Distinguish direct quotations that support or contradict a claim, reasonable inference from existing facts without direct wording, and subjects absent from the records. Identify the source for the first case, explain the reasoning chain for the second, and state uncertainty for the third. Never present an inference as direct source support. Inspired by structured reasoning.
